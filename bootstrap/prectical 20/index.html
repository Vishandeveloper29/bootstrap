City Announces New Smart Transit System

Reported by Daily Herald Staff

Introduction

The city administration has unveiled a new smart public transit initiative aimed at reducing congestion and improving daily commutes for residents.

Main Story

The Smart Transit System includes real-time bus tracking, electronic ticketing, and dedicated bus lanes across major routes. Officials say the system will roll out in three phases over the next 18 months.

View Project Budget

Background

Planning for the transit overhaul began three years ago after a city-wide traffic survey revealed average commute times had increased by 35% over the past decade. The project was approved by the city council last year.
